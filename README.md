# House of Lore Plugin

Social and marketing content for House of Lore med spa in Athens, GA.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| hol-brand | Brand voice (luxe, warm, confident), service menu, Lore Reserve membership details, and med-spa compliance guardrails. Loads automatically for any House of Lore content. |
| social-post | Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok captions, Reel scripts, carousels, stories, and trend adaptations. |
| sms-campaign | SMS blasts, "Reply YES" flows, post-appointment texts, and ManyChat keyword funnels. |
| content-calendar | Weekly or monthly content plans with a balanced mix, seasonal anchors, and a B-roll shot list. |

## Try it

- "Write a Reel script for the Ritz Head Spa"
- "Text blast for Lore Reserve with the DiamondGlow sign-up perk"
- "Build a GLOW keyword funnel for microneedling"
- "Plan House of Lore content for December"

## Keeping it current

Update `skills/hol-brand/references/services.md` and the Lore Reserve section in `skills/hol-brand/SKILL.md` whenever services, prices, or perks change.

Compliance notes are practical marketing guardrails, not legal advice.
