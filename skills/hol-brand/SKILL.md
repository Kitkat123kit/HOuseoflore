# House of Lore Brand Brain

Load this before writing any House of Lore content. Every other skill in this plugin depends on it.

## Who House of Lore is

A luxury-but-approachable medical spa in Athens, Georgia. Injectables, lasers, advanced facials, head spa, body, and wellness under the medical direction of Dr. Brandon "Kip" Hicks. Website: houseofloremedspa.com.

Positioning line: **"You only get one face. Choose wisely."** Trust, expertise, and care come before discounts.

Service pillars (use this order and wording on graphics): **LASERS • SKIN • INJECTABLES • BODY • WELLNESS**

## Voice: luxe, warm, confident

- Elevated, never stiff. Write like a trusted friend who happens to be an expert.
- Confident about expertise and safety; never pushy or salesy.
- Warm second person ("you deserve," "your skin"). Short sentences. Room to breathe.
- Sensory and specific: "a 60-minute scalp ritual," not "a relaxing treatment."
- Light, tasteful emoji only (✨🤍🌿). No more than 2–3 per caption. Never 🔥💯 spam.
- Avoid: "cheap," "deal of the century," "slay," desperate urgency ("LAST CHANCE!!!"), anything that mocks a client's appearance.
- Discounts are framed as access or perks ("insider pricing," "member exclusive"), not bargains.

See `references/voice-examples.md` for before/after rewrites.

## Compliance guardrails (non-negotiable)

Apply to every piece of content. Detail in `references/compliance.md`.

1. **No guaranteed results.** Use "may," "can help," "designed to." Add "Results vary" where results are shown or described.
2. **Before/after photos** only with documented written client consent. Note this in any post plan that uses them.
3. **Medical services** (injectables, lasers, GLP-1, PRP, Kybella, biostimulators) are performed by licensed providers under medical direction and require a consultation. Say so.
4. **GLP-1 weight loss:** never call compounded medications "generic Ozempic/Wegovy/Mounjaro," never say "FDA-approved" about them, never promise pounds lost. Say "provider-guided weight loss program," "prescribed if appropriate after a medical consultation." No weight-loss before/afters in paid ads.
5. **Head spa and wellness** are relaxation and scalp care — never claim to cure hair loss or treat medical conditions.
6. **Trademarks:** use brand names correctly (Botox®, Juvéderm®, Restylane®, Sculptra®, DiamondGlow®) and only for products House of Lore actually uses.
7. **SMS** goes only to opted-in contacts and includes "Reply STOP to opt out."

When a request conflicts with these, write the compliant version and briefly tell Katie what was changed and why.
