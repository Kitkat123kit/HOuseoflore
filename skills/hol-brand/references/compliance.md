# Compliance Detail

Marketing guardrails for a Georgia medical spa. This is a practical checklist, not legal advice; when something is unclear, flag it for Katie and Dr. Hicks.

## Claims language

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| "Erase wrinkles" | "Softens the look of lines" |
| "Guaranteed results" | "Results vary by person" |
| "Lose 30 lbs in 30 days" | "A provider-guided program built around you" |
| "Permanent hair removal" | "Long-lasting hair reduction" |
| "Cures hair loss" | "A restorative scalp ritual" |
| "Painless" | "Most clients describe it as comfortable" |
| "Safe for everyone" | "Your provider will review whether it's right for you" |

## Medical services

- Name that treatments are performed by licensed providers under the medical direction of Dr. Brandon Hicks.
- Every injectable, laser, PRP, Kybella, biostimulator, and weight-loss mention includes "consultation required" or equivalent.
- Do not post dosing, unit counts as promises, or medical advice in comments or DMs. Move clinical questions to a consult.

## GLP-1 / weight loss

- Never say "generic Ozempic," "Ozempic alternative," "same as Mounjaro," or "FDA-approved" for compounded medication.
- No specific weight-loss numbers or timelines.
- Meta ads: no before/after weight images, no body-shaming, target 18+ only, avoid implying the viewer has a weight problem ("Tired of your belly?").
- Price posts say "starting at" and "if prescribed."

## Photos and testimonials

- Before/after and client video only with signed written consent on file.
- Same lighting, angle, and no filters on before/afters. Add "Results vary."
- Testimonials must be real and unedited in meaning; no invented reviews.

## SMS / text marketing

- Send only to contacts who opted in to marketing texts.
- Include the business name and "Reply STOP to opt out."
- Send between 8 a.m. and 9 p.m. Eastern.
- Keep marketing texts separate from appointment reminders.

## Trademarks

- Use ® on first mention of Botox®, Juvéderm®, Restylane®, Sculptra®, Radiesse®, Kybella®, DiamondGlow®, ZO®, Obagi®.
- Don't name a brand House of Lore doesn't carry.
