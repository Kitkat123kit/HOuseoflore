# House of Lore Social Posts

## Before writing

1. Load the `hol-brand` skill and follow its voice and compliance rules.
2. Identify from the request: service or topic, platform(s), format, and goal (bookings, membership, awareness, engagement, event). If the goal is unclear, default to bookings and add a soft Lore Reserve mention.
3. Don't ask questions unless a missing detail would make the content wrong (e.g., an event date or a price). Otherwise write and note assumptions in one line.

## Formats

**Feed caption (Instagram/Facebook)**
- Hook line (first 125 characters must stand alone).
- 2–4 short lines on the experience or benefit, sensory and specific.
- Compliance line where needed ("Consultation required. Results vary.").
- One CTA.
- 5–10 hashtags: mix local (#AthensGA #AthensMedSpa #UGA #ClassicCity), service, and brand (#HouseOfLore #LoreReserve). Facebook gets 0–3 hashtags.

**Reel / TikTok script**
- Hook (0–3 s): on-screen text plus spoken line.
- Beats with timestamps, each paired with a B-roll shot (treatment room close-ups, product pours, provider hands, client reaction, exterior).
- On-screen text for each beat (short, readable without sound).
- Ending CTA and suggested audio vibe (no specific copyrighted song names; describe the mood, e.g., "soft piano trending audio").
- Caption to post with it.
- Keep to 15–45 seconds unless asked.

**Carousel**
- Slide-by-slide copy, 6–8 slides: hook slide, value slides, proof/trust slide, CTA slide.
- Note a visual direction per slide (neutral, cream, gold accents, clean serif headline).

**Story set**
- 3–5 frames with sticker ideas (poll, quiz, question box, link).

## Default deliverable

Unless Katie asks for one thing, deliver:
1. The requested piece, ready to paste.
2. One alternate hook.
3. A one-line posting tip (best time or format note).

Keep it in the chat reply, formatted for easy copying. No preamble.

## Trends

When adapting a trend, keep the House of Lore polish: humor is fine, but never at a client's expense, never mocking appearance, and never undercutting trust in providers. If a trend clashes with the brand, say so and offer a closer-to-brand twist.

## Good recurring angles

- "What really happens at a consult" (trust)
- Provider spotlights and Dr. Hicks' medical direction
- Treatment ASMR (head spa, DiamondGlow)
- Myth vs fact (compliant, educational)
- Member perks and "private beauty account" explainers
- Seasonal skin: summer sun care, fall laser season, holiday glow, New Year reset
- Local Athens moments (game day, graduation, sorority events)
