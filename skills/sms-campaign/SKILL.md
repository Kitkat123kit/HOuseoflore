# House of Lore SMS & Keyword Funnels

## Before writing

1. Load the `hol-brand` skill. Apply its voice and the SMS rules in its compliance reference.
2. Identify the offer, audience (all clients, lapsed, members, new leads, specific service clients), send timing, and goal.

## SMS blast rules

- Start with "House of Lore:" so the sender is clear.
- Under 160 characters when possible; never over 300.
- One offer, one CTA, one link.
- End with "Reply STOP to opt out."
- No ALL CAPS, max one emoji.
- Write 2 versions (A/B): one benefit-led, one urgency- or perk-led (urgency stays calm: "this week," "a few openings Thursday").
- Suggest send day/time (Tue–Thu, 11 a.m.–1 p.m. or 5–7 p.m. Eastern by default).

## "Reply YES" flows

Deliver as a numbered flow:
1. Opening text with the offer and "Reply YES to claim."
2. Auto-reply for YES (booking link or "we'll text you within the hour").
3. Follow-up for no reply after 48 hours (one only).
4. Staff handoff note: what the front desk does next.

Membership sign-up perk flows: the free DiamondGlow is offered only with a Lore Reserve sign-up.

## ManyChat / DM keyword funnels

Deliver:
- **Keyword** (one word, all caps, easy to spell: GLOW, LOCK, READY).
- **Post CTA line** ("Comment GLOW and we'll send you…").
- **DM message 1:** warm thank-you + the promised item (guide, offer, price list).
- **DM message 2:** qualifying question with quick-reply buttons (e.g., "What's your skin goal?" → Texture / Lines / Glow / Not sure).
- **DM message 3 per button:** recommended service + booking link + soft Lore Reserve mention.
- **24-hour follow-up** if no booking.
- Phone/email capture step only with clear consent language for future texts.

## Lapsed-client win-back (common request)

Sequence of 3 texts over 10 days: "we miss you" + personal value, a perk, a final gentle nudge. Never guilt.

## Output

Paste-ready blocks in the reply, labeled by step. Close with one line on how to measure it (replies, bookings, redemptions in Vagaro or GoHighLevel).
