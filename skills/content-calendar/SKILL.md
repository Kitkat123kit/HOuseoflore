# House of Lore Content Calendar

## Before planning

1. Load the `hol-brand` skill.
2. Confirm the window (week, month, or campaign dates). Default to the next full month if unstated.
3. Note anything time-bound in the window: holidays, UGA calendar (game days, move-in, graduation), Mother's Day, Valentine's, gift-card season (Nov–Dec), New Year resets, summer slow season.

## Cadence (default)

- Instagram + Facebook: 5 feed posts/week (at least 2 Reels), daily Stories.
- TikTok: 3–4 videos/week (can repurpose Reels).
- SMS: no more than 2 marketing blasts/month.
- Email: 1–2 per month if Katie wants email included.

## Content mix

| Bucket | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Education & trust | 30% | Consult walkthroughs, provider spotlights, myth vs fact |
| Treatment showcase | 30% | Head spa ASMR, DiamondGlow, lip flip, laser |
| Membership & offers | 20% | Lore Reserve explainers, referral perk, seasonal offers |
| Community & personality | 20% | Athens moments, team, behind the scenes, trends |

Every week includes at least one Lore Reserve touchpoint.

## Seasonal anchors

- **Jan:** New Year reset, weight-loss program (compliant), skin reset.
- **Feb:** Valentine's, lip flips and glow.
- **Mar–Apr:** pre-summer skin, laser hair removal start-now push.
- **May:** Mother's Day gift cards, graduation glow.
- **Jun–Aug (slow season):** head spa self-care, member-exclusive perks, events and partnerships (boutique co-hosts, sorority nights), referral pushes rather than deep discounts.
- **Sep–Oct:** fall laser and peel season, game-day glam.
- **Nov–Dec:** gift cards (instant online delivery, men-buying-for-women angle, last-minute urgency), holiday party glow.

## Output

Build the calendar as a table: Date | Platform | Format | Topic/hook | Goal | Offer/CTA | B-roll needed.

- Under ~3 weeks: deliver the table in the chat reply.
- A full month or more: offer to put it in a spreadsheet Katie can keep and edit.

After the table, list:
1. Shots to capture this period (for the B-roll vault).
2. The 2–3 posts to write first. Offer to draft them with the `social-post` skill and any texts with `sms-campaign`.
